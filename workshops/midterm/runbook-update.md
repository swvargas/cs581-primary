## Controls to add or change

### Approval and evidence gates

1. **Preserve before alteration, when safe:** EDGC owner documents the scenario's distinct write/discovery times and requests preservation of existing raw register responses, journals, SIEM exports, collector receipt metadata, source/configuration/service versions, clock status and access inventories. Authorized custodians preserve originals, acquisition/source details, hashes and restricted copies outside the suspect trust domain; redacted working copies must not contain credentials/private keys. Hashes protect later comparison, not truth of already compromised data. W5-R:254-264 states the owner lacks sudo for packet capture; approved administrator/network evidence is needed, not invented elevation. No collection is claimed here.
2. **Safety/authority gate:** Immediately refer suspected train unavailability and any maintenance-risk decision to authorized shift operations and engineering; refer security containment to the cybersecurity lead and affected owners. Evaluate reporting in parallel through authorized reporting personnel. Evidence preservation must not delay an immediate action necessary for plant safety; document what could not safely be preserved. A classmate/runbook editor is not authorized to clear a control state, declare operability or choose plant shutdown actions (M:5,34-35; W5-R:272-274).
3. **Before disruptive containment/restoration:** Require a recorded scope, safety-function/dependency review, approver, maintenance/work authorization, preserved pre-change state where feasible, trusted access path, rollback/abort plan and acceptance criteria. Include effects on monitoring/credited control before disabling a connector, revoking credentials, restricting communications or changing services. No reset, restart, inhibit clear, fuel-value write or EDG start test is the default response.
4. **Validate then release:** Use offline/replay/test-instance validation first. Any plant functional surveillance or restoration test requires the applicable approved procedure, authorized operations/engineering supervision, safe plant conditions and documented results. Return-to-service requires independent function/operability assessment and security evidence of controlled access/clean configuration; green summary, restarted service or completed notification alone does not close the incident. W5 historical changes at W5-R:317-328 are not new incident remediation.

## Fix, accept, or defer

**Decision ledger:** D1-D6 and C1-C3 are proposed fixes, with deployment deferred until their authority, map, safety and validation gates pass. EDGC owner can draft point-map/trust/response corrections and detection requirements now; elevated acquisition, host/service enforcement and retention infrastructure need administrators; connector/RMS/network/credential changes need those owners and approvals. C4 accepts only the explicitly unmodeled simulator register with no physical credit, fixes misleading interpretation and defers real fuel instrumentation/model expansion. C5 fixes escalation/evidence criteria while deferring plant intervention to authorized operations. No deferred item is a reason to wait on an already applicable safety/security response or reporting duty.

**Remaining risks:** Request auditing or polling can miss off-path/rapid changes; same-source compromise can produce internally consistent lies; receipt-locked logs do not authenticate source truth; an allowed maintenance path or writable backend can bypass a new gateway; upstream persistence and current credential scope remain unresolved. Physical train operability, complete state history, summary conversion and full plant TS/mode applicability remain missing. Acceptance of lab-model limitations is bounded to the exercise, not a declaration that unknown standby readiness is acceptable for a real plant.

**Closeout evidence required, not achieved:** Approved change records, offline validation results, demonstrated trusted collection/retention/notification delivery, evidence of blocked unapproved writes across all relevant paths, cross-owner connector containment records, and operations/engineering's independent operability/return-to-service determination. Keep proposed, approved, implemented and verified statuses separate in the successor handoff. No implementation or validation result is recorded by this proposal.
